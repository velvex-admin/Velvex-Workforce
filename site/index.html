Diagnostic Methodology

Four stages.
One verdict.

Every enterprise passed through Velvex moves through a fixed sequence — screened, read, scored, and assigned a health state — performed end to end by Veĺa, our proprietary diagnostic engine.

I

Eligibility Review

Before any diagnostic proceeds, the engagement is screened across three layers — industry legitimacy, operational integrity, and representation. A business that cannot clear all three is not scored.

→ Three-layer screen
II

Structural Reading

Veĺa reads the business as seven connected engines — value, demand, delivery, trust, cashflow, adaptation, and exposure — and names the tensions between them. This is where what is load-bearing is separated from what is merely visible.

→ Seven engines, read as one chain
III

Survivability Scoring

Six defined dimensions — market position, offer structure, customer acquisition, conversion systems, operations, and growth leverage — are rated on survivability under weightings validated by analytic hierarchy process, resolving to a single Final Velvex Score out of 100.

→ Six dimensions, one score
IV

Health State

The score resolves into a defined health state — the band that states, without euphemism, how much structural pressure the business is carrying and whether that pressure is survivable at scale.

→ Final verdict

Executive Output · Velvex Standard

The Executive Ledger

The Velvex process produces a singular, definitive Executive Ledger — an immutable, third-party operational health assessment carrying a seven-engine structural reading, a six-dimension survivability score, a defined health state, ranked pressure points, and three targeted recommendations. It is not an ongoing strategy engagement. It is the cold, unvarnished structural truth about where your operation holds and exactly where it is leaking efficiency — plus concrete direction on what to do about it.

Version-controlled  ·  Institutional Distribution  ·  Velvex Standard v1.0
After The Ledger

The diagnostic ends.
Nothing is sold behind it.

What does not happen after the Ledger
—

No delivery engagement. The three prioritised recommendations are executed by you, or by anyone you choose. Never by Velvex.

—

No advisor referral. Velvex operates no partner network and takes no fee for an introduction.

—

No fee credited toward downstream work. The engagement price buys the Ledger, because there is nothing behind it to credit a fee against.

—

No retainer, no subscription, no standing advisory relationship.

Why that is structural, not a policy
→

Velvex has no delivery arm. We do not sell the work the Ledger recommends, and we are paid by no one who does.

→

A diagnostic run by a provider carrying delivery revenue cannot be terminal even when it is honest, because the finding that recommends more work is the finding that pays them.

→

Structured follow-up at 30, 90 and 180 days is a check on the Ledger, not a next step. It verifies whether the structural reading held. It does not open a new engagement.

→

The money-back guarantee sits on the Ledger itself, not on work Velvex is paid to deliver afterwards.
